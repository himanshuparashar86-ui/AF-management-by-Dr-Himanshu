# Clinical references — AF Management v1.4

## Single guideline framework
**2024 ESC Guidelines for the management of atrial fibrillation** (AF-CARE framework).  
Official ESC guideline page: https://www.escardio.org/guidelines/clinical-practice-guidelines/all-esc-practice-guidelines/atrial-fibrillation  
Full guideline: https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae176

The app uses this guideline only for AF stroke-risk assessment, anticoagulation and cardioversion timing. No parallel AHA/ACC AF pathway is displayed.

Key pathway points:
- CHA₂DS₂-VA 0: OAC generally not recommended; 1: consider OAC; ≥2: OAC recommended, subject to contraindications and clinical assessment.
- If AF duration is >24 h or unknown, do not perform elective cardioversion unless at least 3 weeks of therapeutic anticoagulation has been given or TOE/TEE excludes intracardiac thrombus.
- If atrial thrombus is detected, defer elective cardioversion, institute therapeutic anticoagulation and repeat TOE/TEE after the treatment interval.
- Most patients should continue OAC for at least 4 weeks after cardioversion. OAC may be omitted only in patients without thromboembolic risk factors when sinus rhythm is restored within 24 h of AF onset.
- In patients with thromboembolic risk factors, long-term OAC is based on risk regardless of apparent rhythm outcome.
- In acute or worsening haemodynamic instability thought to be caused by AF, rapid electrical cardioversion is recommended.

## Cockcroft–Gault renal function
CrCl (mL/min) = ((140 − age) × weight in kg × 0.85 if female) / (72 × serum creatinine in mg/dL).

Use clinical judgement for obesity, oedema, frailty, unstable renal function, dialysis and other extremes. Confirm the appropriate body weight and renal assessment with pharmacy/local policy.

## Anticoagulant product information
The dose module is an informational reference only and currently uses US product-label criteria for selected DOACs. Verify current locally approved Indian product information for the exact brand/generic, renal and hepatic function, age/weight criteria, interactions, contraindications and formulary before prescribing. Warfarin dosing must be individualized and INR-guided.

## Cardioversion energy
The 2024 ESC AF guideline does not provide a single universal joule setting for synchronized electrical cardioversion. Select energy according to the actual defibrillator manufacturer's instructions, waveform, pad configuration and the hospital-approved cardioversion protocol. Ensure synchronization before every shock. This ESC-only release does not import an AHA energy recommendation.
