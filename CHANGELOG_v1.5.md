# Version 1.5 — Clinical display and doctor-order updates

- Doctor decision question about AF-attributable haemodynamic instability is shown only when the entered assessment includes a qualifying instability sign (severe hypotension / low MAP or a bedside clinical instability red flag). Isolated low SpO₂ and ECG findings alone do not show this attribution question.
- Staff Mode shows DC cardioversion preparation guidance only when possible AF-related clinical instability is present. Stable AF does not display shock/cardioversion terminology.
- AF on ECG alone is not a red flag. Wide QRS / confirmed pre-excitation and low oxygen saturation have separate urgent-review / respiratory guidance without automatically implying cardioversion.
- Doctor anticoagulant selector includes Apixaban, Rivaroxaban, Dabigatran, Warfarin, Acitrom (acenocoumarol), IV unfractionated heparin and Enoxaparin (Clexane). No injectable dose is automatically generated.
- Treatment-order builder updated with the same anticoagulant set and expanded investigations: ECG, electrolytes, echocardiography, TFT, CBC, KFT, ABG, Troponin I and NT-proBNP.
- Rate-control and rhythm-control lists retained as requested.
- Existing v1.4 staff wizard, mandatory field gates, emergency contacts, Doctor Mode record editing/deletion, treatment builder, favourites, approval/share and backup/restore are retained.

Clinical review required before use with real patients. Local product labels, dose protocols, anticoagulation pathways, and hospital emergency/cardioversion SOPs must be verified by the responsible clinical governance team.
