# Version 1.0 — Staff workflow update

- Staff-facing instructions simplified into short, plain Hindi/Hinglish actions.
- Red/orange/green staff alerts use action-oriented wording.
- After doctor approval, the doctor selects the preparation pathway: DC cardioversion or medical management.
- Staff receives only the matching preparation checklist.
- Staff can tick checklist tasks and save completion; staff name and timestamp are added to record history.
- Staff is reminded not to independently choose medicines, doses, cardioversion or shock delivery.
- Deterioration requires immediate hospital emergency escalation, not waiting for app/WhatsApp.
- ESC 2024 remains the only AF guideline framework in this package.

Important: The app remains a local-browser prototype. Role selection is not secure authentication; records are not synchronized across devices, and JSON exports are not encrypted. Do not use real patient identifiers until secure authentication, access control, encryption, audit, backup and approved hospital governance are implemented.
